Our Story

Built by a father. For his son.

"I wanted GL to hear my voice telling her stories, even when I couldn't be there."

Casa Companion started as one dad's weekend project. A cloned voice inside a stuffed crow, so his baby boy could hear bedtime stories in his father's voice anytime.

It grew into something bigger. A platform that helps families connect, learn together, preserve their heritage, and capture the magic of those first 1,000 days.

Peter had already cloned his voice for his nephews Liam and Logan so they could hear Uncle Pete read stories even when he was miles away. When his son Gian Lucca arrived on July 27, 2025, he built more. Stories in Italian, because language is heritage and heritage disappears in one generation if you let it. Baby sign language, because the research is clear. Music. Games. A whole ecosystem of things a father builds when he can't be in two places at once.

Casa Companion is that ecosystem made physical. A toy your child can hold, squeeze, sleep with. A toy that speaks in your voice. That teaches in three languages. That remembers every milestone. That never requires a screen.

It's Teddy Ruxpin's emotional bond. Cabbage Patch's personalization. Furby's personality. But it's yours. Literally yours. Your voice, your family's stories, your child's name in every tale.

The Anti-Screen-Time Toy

71% of parents are concerned about screen time. Casa Companion replaces screens with story time. No display. No doom scrolling. Just a soft companion that speaks in the voice your child trusts most. Yours.
