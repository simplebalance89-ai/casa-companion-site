## GENERATION NOTES

- **Azure deployment:** `casa-dalle-sweden` (DALL-E 3)
- **Resolution:** 1024x1024 for social, 1792x1024 for landscape marketing/lifestyle
- **Consistency rule:** Every plush should look premium, soft, about 12 inches, with subtle glowing eyes (amber for crow, golden for owl, warm brown for bear, etc.)
- **NO copyrighted characters.** No Disney, no branded toys in background.
- **Color palette reference:** Warm cream #FAF7F2, gold #D4A017, dark #0D0D1A
