The Origin Story

Built by a father. For his son. For every family.

"My mother died of cancer when I was 17. I have maybe 10 memories of her voice. If I could have had a toy that spoke in her voice when I was a kid, I would have held onto it for the rest of my life."

— Peter, Founder of Casa Companion

Casa Companion started as a weekend project. Peter builds AI for business. When his son Gian Lucca was born on July 27, 2025, he wanted something that didn't exist: a toy that could speak in his own voice, tell stories in Italian (the way his mother would have), and grow with his child from day one.

He let his nephews Liam and Logan try the prototype. Two and four years old. They wouldn't put it down. That's when he knew this wasn't just for his family.

"I built this so my son could hear me — even when I'm not in the room."

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions

Is Casa Companion safe for infants and toddlers?

Yes. Casa Companion is designed for ages 0–5 and beyond. All materials are CPSIA compliant and hypoallergenic. Volume is hard-capped at 85dB (pediatrician-recommended). All magnets are fully encased. The plush meets ASTM F963-23 toy safety standards. There is no screen and no camera on the device.

How does voice cloning work? Is my voice data safe?

You read 12 short phrases in the Capo app. Our AI creates a voice model in about 5 minutes. Voice recordings are encrypted end-to-end and stored securely. We never sell voice data. You can delete your voice profile at any time. The voice clone is used only within your family's account.

Is it recording my child?

Casa Companion only listens when your child actively engages it (squeeze or tap). It does not passively record. There is no always-on microphone. All conversation data is processed in real-time and only conversation summaries are stored (not raw audio). We are COPPA 2025 compliant.

What happens if the company shuts down?

We learned from Moxie's failure. Voice recordings are stored locally on your device, not just in the cloud. The companion has an offline mode with cached stories and voice profiles. If Casa Companion ever shuts down, your voice data remains yours. We will never brick your toy.

What age range is this for?

Casa Companion is designed for ages 0–5 as the primary range, but grows with your child beyond that. For infants, it's a voice-activated lullaby and story machine. For toddlers, it's an interactive conversation partner. For preschoolers, it's a learning companion with STEM, language, and social-emotional content.

How long does the battery last?

6–8 hours of active use on a full charge (3.7V 2500mAh LiPo battery). The magnetic charging dock makes it easy: just set the companion in its nest and it charges. USB-C input. LED eyes show charging status.

Can multiple family members clone their voice?

Yes. The number of voice profiles depends on your plan. The Early Bird tier includes 1 voice profile. The Backer Exclusive includes 3 (Mom, Dad, Grandma). The Grandparent Bundle includes unlimited voice profiles. Each family member records their own 12 phrases in the Capo app.

What languages does it support?

At launch: English, Italian, and Spanish. Heritage language learning is built in from day one. Native-level pronunciation and cultural stories. Stretch goals will add French, Mandarin, Portuguese, Polish, and more. The companion seamlessly switches between languages.

Is there a subscription? What's free?

The companion works out of the box with a basic free tier: 1 voice profile, English stories, core learning activities. Family ($4.99/mo) unlocks 3 languages, 3 voice profiles, and all learning modes. Premium ($9.99/mo) adds unlimited voices and grandparent features. All Kickstarter backers get 6–12 months included. Voice recordings never expire, regardless of subscription status.

When will backers receive their companions?

Kickstarter launches May 5, 2026. Expected delivery is November 2026, in time for the holiday season. We will provide regular backer updates with manufacturing progress, photos, and timeline updates throughout the process.
