The Story
Built by a father.
For his daughter.
"I wanted GL to hear my voice telling her stories, even when I couldn't be there."

Casa Companion started as one dad's weekend project. A cloned voice inside a stuffed crow, so his baby girl could hear bedtime stories in her father's voice anytime.

It grew into something bigger. A platform that helps families connect, learn together, preserve their heritage, and capture the magic of those first 1,000 days.

Now we want every family to have this.

What It Does
More than a toy. A family companion.
Voice-cloned AI that grows with your child through the first 1,000 days and beyond.
🎤

Your Voice, Always There

Clone your voice so your child hears YOU telling stories, singing lullabies, teaching words. Even when you're at work or traveling.

🌍

Three Languages

English, Italian, and Spanish. Trilingual vocab, pronunciation, and cultural stories. Heritage preserved through play.

📸

Squeeze to Snap

Squeeze the paw and your iPhone takes a photo. Moments captured and saved to your family app automatically.

🌱

Grows With Them

Content evolves as your child develops. Milestones tracked, activities suggested, difficulty adapts. From newborn to preschool.

☀️

Solar Charging Nest

Place your companion in its themed nest to charge. Solar panel on top keeps it powered by sunlight. Magnetic, effortless.

🔊

Plug In Anywhere

3.5mm aux jack for car rides, headphones for quiet time, Bluetooth for the living room speaker. The toy is the brain, any speaker is the voice.

📦

Monthly Updates

New stories, seasonal content, language packs, and milestone activities pushed over WiFi while your companion sleeps in its nest.

🛁

Machine Washable

Remove the electronics pod (2 seconds), toss the plush in the wash. Because kids are kids.

🛡️

Safe by Design

Volume-capped at 85dB. No camera. No data sold. COPPA compliant. ASTM F963 toy safety certified. Built by a parent, for parents.

1

Unbox

2

4

Play
